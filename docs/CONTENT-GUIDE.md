# CONTENT GUIDE: how pages are written

## Voice
- Clear, confident, helpful. Like a well-informed friend, not a salesperson.
- Plain US English. Short sentences. No hype words like "amazing" or "unbeatable".
- Give a clear pick and the reason for it.

## Honesty rules
- Disclose affiliate links near the top of the page.
- Only state prices and discounts that are real and current; show "Last updated".
- No fake urgency ("only 2 left!") and no invented reviews or ratings.
- Say who a product is not for.

## Standard disclosure text
"We may earn a commission if you buy through links on this page, at no extra cost to you. This doesn't affect our picks."

## Page templates

### 1. Best-of roundup ("Best Dive Watches Under $300")
1. H1, disclosure, 2-sentence intro answering the query
2. Quick picks table (best overall, best budget, best premium)
3. Detailed pick sections: image, who it's for, pros, cons, price, "View deal"
4. How we chose
5. Buying guide (3-5 things to look for)
6. FAQ (3-5 real questions)
7. Related pages

### 2. Versus ("Rolex vs Omega")
1. H1, disclosure, short verdict up front
2. Side-by-side comparison table
3. Sections: price, quality, resale value, style, who should buy which
4. Final verdict, FAQ, related pages

### 3. Review / "Is it worth it?"
1. H1, disclosure, verdict
2. Pros and cons, who it's for, who should skip it
3. Alternatives, price and where to buy, FAQ

### 4. Category landing page
- Intro (100-200 words), featured pages grid, links to subtopics

## Checklist before publishing
- [ ] Meta title (<60) and description (<160) written
- [ ] Slug set, category chosen
- [ ] Disclosure present
- [ ] All images have alt text
- [ ] All affiliate links use `/go/<slug>`
- [ ] 2-3 internal links added
- [ ] Facts and prices checked
- [ ] Status set to published
